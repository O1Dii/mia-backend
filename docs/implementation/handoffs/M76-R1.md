## Outcome

Choosing another chat always shows that chat's feed, however fast the taps and whichever load answers last. Tool steps, failure reasons and
confirmations are chips in one horizontal row, collapsed until tapped; the conversation stays in view after one or several confirmations. After
reopening a chat (or for a confirmation decided on another device) steps and decided confirmations come from `FeedItem.steps`. The automatic
speech after an answer reads the same text as «Озвучить».

## Root cause of the switching bug

The chat list only set the shared `ChatSelection` and popped the back stack; the chat feed reloaded in `ChatRoute`'s
`LaunchedEffect(presenter) { presenter.onShown() }`, i.e. only when the chat screen **entered the composition again**. The title is bound to the
selection directly, so it always changed. With a quick tap in the list the pop happens before the chat's exit transition has finished: the chat
screen never left the composition, the effect did not run, the feed stayed. Reproduced with a Robolectric `NavHost` probe (effect ran once instead
of twice when the pop came within the transition; twice after waiting). M76 tests missed it because they called `openChat()`/`onShown()` directly.
Second weakness found: loads were guarded by `shownTarget != asked` (equality of targets with titles), "older pages" and refresh were not
cancelled on a switch, so a late answer of a chat left behind could land in the new one.

## Scope completed

- `ChatPresenter` follows `ChatSelection.target` (collector → `syncToSelection()`), independent of the Compose lifecycle; generation counter drops
  stale first/older/refresh loads and failures; older/refresh jobs cancelled on switch; the self-made selection change while a new dialog creates
  its chat (`creatingChat`) does not wipe the question, and a different chat chosen meanwhile wins afterwards.
- Steps and confirmations UI: `ToolStepsView` = horizontal chip row (tool · status), one open step below it (arguments, reason, confirmation);
  pending confirmation chip is accented («ждёт решения») and opens the full card with Approve/Deny; decided ones open as a record (outcome, where
  decided: на телефоне / в вебе / в Telegram). Confirmations of this session sit inside their step (by `run_id` + `tool_call_id`); those without
  a step become chips of their own and consecutive ones share one row (`FeedRow.group`).
- `FeedItem.steps` parsing (`FeedJson.steps`): `ToolStep` gained `pluginId/risk/executor/approval`; `ToolStepStatus` has the seven wire statuses
  (`fromWire`: unknown → failure); `StepApproval`. Feed steps win over the session journal; a live decision on this phone overrides them.
- Auto-speech: `ChatPresenter.spokenText(text)` is used by `onFinal` and `toggleSpeak`; response `speech` only as fallback for empty text.

## User re-check on the phone (UC-MW5b-live)

1. Open «Чаты», tap another chat at once (do not wait for the animation), repeat A → B → C quickly: the feed must be the chat you ended on, title matches.
2. Ask something that calls a tool (e.g. a timer or a search): under the answer there is a row of chips «инструмент · готово»; nothing is open; tap a chip → details.
3. Trigger a confirmation (Linear «создай задачу»): a chip «… ждёт решения» appears, the question stays visible; tap → card → Подтвердить. Do two in a row: still one row.
4. Reopen the chat (or decide on the web): the chips are still there; the confirmation shows «Одобрено · Решено в вебе/на телефоне». (Needs backend M74-R1 deployed.)
5. Dictate a question whose answer has several paragraphs: the whole answer is read, same as «Озвучить».

## Known limitations

- Until M74-R1 is deployed the feed has no `steps`: after reopening only chips of the current app session exist (as before).
- A pending confirmation is not auto-expanded (plan: collapsed); revisit if missed (OI-M76R1-002).
