## Outcome

Voice mode no longer hears its own answer. On SM-S938B with the real loudspeaker and microphone, 6 answers of
10.6–17 s (the server caps voice speech at 250 chars) at the phone's current volume: **0 false barge-ins, 0 false speech
starts, 0 phantom turns**; the pre-fix pipeline on the same stand: **100 false barge-ins in 2 answers, never settled**
(reproduces the user report). Speech injected into the real echo-cancelled microphone signal during an answer interrupts
it 3/3 at two levels: VAD speech start → `tts.stop()` 161–171 ms, first voiced sample → stop 282–289 ms. STT through
speaker → mic on the new path: 19/20 exact, WER 2.3 % (old path at the same media volume: 14/20, WER 14 %).
A real person talking over the answer could not be automated (see Known limitations) → UC-R-0110.

## Root cause (confirmed on the device)

System TTS on `USAGE_ASSISTANT` + `VOICE_RECOGNITION` capture: echo at the mic during playback p90 0.11 / max 0.17 RMS,
just after it up to 0.29, vs the 0.075 playback threshold that also dropped to 0.025 the moment TTS said `Done`.
Probe (`AcousticProbeTest`, `results-m22r1/probe-variants.json`): the platform echo canceller works **only in
`MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION`** (`VOICE_COMMUNICATION` source + AEC); in `MODE_NORMAL` nothing is cancelled for any source/usage,
and `USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION` in `MODE_NORMAL` plays on the earpiece.

## Known limitations (read before UC-R-0110)

- **Human barge-in is unverified.** In `MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION` the canceller's reference is everything the loudspeaker
  plays while a voice stream is open (a silent voice stream already cancels a media phrase completely), so no sound played
  by the phone can stand in for a person. Whether Samsung's VoIP suppressor lets a real voice through while the answer
  plays (double talk) is unknown → OI-M22-R1-001. If it does not, voice barge-in works only between answers; Stop stays.
- Answers in voice mode play at the **call volume** (speaker 15/15 here) instead of the assistant/media volume; volume keys
  in voice mode change it. Measured mic level of the answer is similar (p90 0.38 vs 0.31) → OI-M22-R1-002.
- Headsets (wired/BT) and other devices untested → OI-M22-R1-003. Without a working platform canceller the adaptive
  2× echo margin prevents self-interruption but makes barge-in during playback hard.
- No barge-in during the first 400 ms of an answer (after silence); sentence boundaries do not re-arm it.
- M22's `CascadeDeviceGateTest` (injected mic, system TTS) was not rerun; its barge-ins happen ≥ 300 ms after playback starts.
- Gemini Live (hidden) unchanged except the larger pre-roll it sends.

## Decisions

- Communication path for voice mode only; chat dictation, wake word and Live keep `RECOGNITION`; chat «Озвучить» keeps
  the system TTS. Defensive VAD measures stay on top of the canceller (cheap; protect devices with a weak one).
- Floor 0.04 RMS during playback: residual echo max 0.017, idle p99 0.02, AGC'd speech ≈ 0.1.
- Pre-roll fix lives in the VAD (owner) so every consumer gets it; no change to `SpeechSegmentAssembler`.
