# S31 handoff

## Outcome

Voice was not reachable in the app at all: the voice destination rendered a
placeholder, so S25's controller and S26's barge-in had never run inside the
product. After wiring the destination and fixing three blocking defects, one
complete round trip was observed on a physical SM-S938B — device microphone →
backend → real Gemini Live → response transcripts and audio frames back on the
device. **The 20-turn soak, the interruption gate, the reconnect gate and every
latency figure remain unmeasured**, so S31 is PARTIAL.

## Scope completed

- Voice destination wired into the app: `AppContainer.voiceController`,
  `VoiceDestination`, optional conversation argument on the voice route,
  microphone permission request, controller lifetime owned by the destination.
- Debug-only loopback cleartext policy so a USB-attached device reaches a
  developer backend through `adb reverse`.
- Three blocking defects found and repaired: [S17-R1](S17-R1.md),
  [S16-R1](S16-R1.md), [S25-R1](S25-R1.md).
- Real Gemini Live verified against the live API with tools attached and with
  injected 16 kHz speech.

## What was actually observed on the device

One full round trip, at Android head `dfd37c4` with the S16-R1 backend:

~~~text
session.ready
input_audio.speech_started / speech_stopped
transcript.input.delta          <- Gemini transcribed the spoken question
response.started
transcript.output.delta ×13     <- the answer
response.audio.started
response.audio.completed
transcript.output.final
transcript.input.final
turn.completed / usage.updated
~~~

Uplink audio levels measured at the provider boundary during that run: room
noise RMS ≈ 55–80, speech RMS ≈ 1010, peak 2714 of 32767.

## What was NOT verified

- **Audible playback.** That round trip still carried the S25-R1 defect, so
  playback aborted. After the fix, acoustic capture was too weak to elicit a
  further response, so no turn has been heard end to end.
- 20-turn soak, interruption/barge-in against cloud audio, network
  disconnect/reconnect, latency measurement, Bluetooth cases.
- Voice turns persisting into conversation history.

## Decisions

- Wiring the voice destination is integration work on integrator-owned app files
  (`MiaApp.kt`, `AppContainer.kt`, `MiaRoutes.kt`), so it was done here rather
  than deferred: without it S31 has nothing to test.
- The three defects were repaired inside W6 as named repair sessions rather than
  deferred, because each one blocked every remaining W6 gate. Precedent:
  [S21-R2](S21-R2.md).
- `adb reverse` over USB replaced the public HTTPS tunnel S21-R2 used. It needs
  no external ingress; the client already derives `ws://` from an `http://` base
  URL with a matching origin.
- The acoustic gate was driven by macOS speech synthesis through the Mac
  speakers into the phone microphone. The operator elected to close S31 without
  completing it; see Blockers.

## Blockers

Acoustic coupling. The phone captured synthesized speech at roughly 8 % of full
scale (peak 2714/32767), which its own VAD detects but Gemini does not reliably
treat as speech. Closing the remaining gates needs the phone within ~10–20 cm of
the sound source, or a person speaking to it directly. This is physical setup,
not a code defect: `MediaRecorder.AudioSource.VOICE_RECOGNITION` is already the
correct source, and the same pipeline succeeds with injected audio at normal
levels.

## Known limitations

- OI-W6-008 and OI-W6-009, both recorded here, were repaired straight afterwards
  in [S15-R1](S15-R1.md), together with a third defect the same round exposed:
  ordinary room noise overflowed the pre-commit buffer and ended the session in
  about 30 s. A silent session now holds 180 s on the device.
- The underlying mismatch remains: MIA's contract is client-commit-driven while
  the Live setup leaves Gemini's automatic activity detection on, so two VADs
  compete. S31-R1 owns that, because only speech can judge the fix.
- The Android client does not surface a closed socket while waiting for a
  response (OI-W6-016).
