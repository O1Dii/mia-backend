## Outcome

All three live symptoms are fixed and reproduced/verified on SM-S938B (Android 16):

- (a) Keyboard: MainActivity had no `windowSoftInputMode`; with `enableEdgeToEdge()` the system panned the window by the
  keyboard height on top of `MiaScreen`'s `safeDrawing` (IME) padding. Measured before (same window without a mode):
  composer at y=313–366 px of 2340, title at −750 px, 238 px of feed left. After (`adjustResize`, insets handled once in
  MiaScreen): composer 75 px above the keyboard top (1332 px), title on screen, 852 px of feed, newest message visible; the
  feed scrolls to the latest message when the keyboard opens. Same in the real app (MainActivity, live build): composer
  bottom 1257 / keyboard top 1332, 695 px of feed under the web chips.
- (b) Dictation: one dictation now runs from the tap until stop or 3.5 s of quiet after speech. A 1.4 s pause closes a
  piece of speech that is decoded while listening goes on (text grows in the field); shorter pauses stay in the same piece.
  Each piece starts 400 ms before the VAD trigger (own ring buffer, independent of the VAD pre-roll that M22-R1 fixes).
  Stop keeps reading until frames captured up to the tap + 240 ms tail arrived (bounded by 800 ms), so queued audio and
  the end of the last word are decoded; quiet speech the VAD never triggered on is decoded on stop. The microphone opens
  before `stt.prepare()` (runs in parallel; first call hashes + loads the model). Auto-send waits for the whole dictation.
- (c) Side key: "Чат" is always on the voice screen. Under the voice screen there is a chat → pop back; voice is the
  assistant's own task (created by an assistant launch) → `AssistantIntents.openChat` opens the chat in the app's normal
  task (CLEAR_TOP|SINGLE_TOP → `onNewIntent`) and the assistant activity closes once covered (onStop) or after 1 s — unless
  the intent came back to the same activity (then it is the normal task: it shows the chat and stays); over the lock screen
  the credential is asked first and the exit continues after unlock (M20 policy kept; cancel closes the screen).
  A repeated press while voice is shown keeps the running voice session (unchanged); over the lock screen with entries
  under voice the stack is reduced to voice only.

## Decisions

- Dictation segments itself (its own silence counter and pre-roll ring) instead of relying on `VadConfig.endFrames` (max 25 =
  1.0 s) or the VAD pre-roll: no `core/audio` change needed; the VAD is used for start detection and level only.
- The chat in the assistant task would be a second chat instance outside recents; the hand-over to the normal task gives the
  "normal app" the user asked for. The guard (`AssistantHandOver`) prevents closing the app when the system delivered the
  assistant launch to the standard task (observed on One UI).
- Repeated side-key press while voice is on screen does not restart the voice session (an approval or a speaking answer is
  not dropped).

## User check notes for UC-R-0110 (integrator owns USER_CHECKS.md)

- Keyboard: in the chat tap the field: the field sits right on the keyboard, the top bar and the latest messages stay visible.
- Dictation: say a phrase with pauses up to ~1.5 s: the text grows in the field after each longer pause and the mic keeps
  listening; it stops by itself ~3.5 s after you finish, or tap the mic to stop (the last word must be there); the first word
  must not lose its start; the mic indicator should appear at once (model load runs in parallel).
- Side key, unlocked phone: close the app (swipe from recents), press the side key → voice screen with «Чат» → tap → the chat
  opens in the normal app (appears in recents; the launcher icon returns to it). Repeat with the app open: «Чат» returns to it.
- Side key, locked phone: «Чат» asks for the PIN/biometrics first; after unlocking the chat opens.
