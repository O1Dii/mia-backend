# M75-R1 handoff

Branch `session/M75-R1-web-compact-steps` (worktree `mia-helper-wt/M75-R1`), base `6ac34c0`. Web only (`web/src`, `web/tests`); no
backend code, migration or contract change. Nothing merged or pushed. Built **against the `FeedItem.steps` contract** of the plan
(M74-R1 runs in parallel and was not visible): the feed case is tested with a mocked `GET /v1/feed`.

## Outcome

- **Journal is compact.** Tool calls, «Почему не получилось» and decided approvals are chips (status icon + short tool name) in one
  horizontally scrolling row; a tap opens the details under the row (reason, redacted arguments, approval and who decided it). Nothing
  is open by default. A pending approval stays actionable as a short bar (summary, risk, status, «Одобрить/Отклонить», ~170 px) and
  also shows as a chip; after the decision the bar folds into the chip. At 390 px the question, the bar and the composer stay in view
  after one and after three approvals.
- **History from the feed.** `FeedItem.steps` → `ToolStep` (`stepFromFeed`): after a reload and on another device chips, reasons,
  arguments and decided approvals appear; an approval decided on the phone (`channel: android`) reads «Одобрено · на телефоне».
  An approval with `device_consent` (M70) shows «Подтвердите на телефоне» without buttons (chat bar and «Подтверждения» screen).
- **Read-aloud.** Root cause: `response.speech` is the short line (clipped to 250 characters by the server, `run_voice_speech_max_chars`),
  the full answer is in `display`. Automatic reading used `speech`; the button after a reload used the feed text (display) — so «first
  paragraph vs all». Now one function, `spokenText` (`lib/chat.tsx`), serves both.
- **Inbox.** The screen drew `response.speech` even for `display.kind=text` (`DisplayView` takes its text from the argument), so a
  report was one phrase. `ResultView` shows `textOfDisplay(display, speech)` folded to ~6 lines with «Показать полностью / Свернуть»
  (inbox and task list).

## Assumptions about M74-R1 (for the integrator)

1. `FeedItem.steps[]` exactly as in the plan: `{tool_call_id, tool, plugin_id?, risk?, executor?, status, arguments?, error_code?,
   error_detail?, approval?{id,status,summary,decided_at?,channel?,device_id?}}`; `risk` missing → treated as `read`. `error_code` is
   shown in words (`errorText`) when `error_detail` is absent.
2. Full result text of a task: **no new field/endpoint assumed**. The client reads `response.display` (text/card markdown, app
   fallback) else `response.speech` from `GET /v1/inbox` and `GET /v1/jobs`. If M74-R1 names another field or a `GET`, change the
   single `text` line in `ResultView` (`components/Inbox.tsx`). If the inbox still carries only the short `speech` with `display: null`,
   the card shows that and no «Показать полностью» (it appears only when the text overflows).
3. A pending approval that comes from the feed has no `args_hash`/`arguments`, so it cannot be decided from a historical answer: the
   chat shows «Ждёт вашего решения … Открыть подтверждения». Live runs still carry the full approval (`approval.requested`).
   Asking M74-R1 to add `arguments`/`args_hash` to `FeedStep.approval` would lift this (OI-M75-R1-003).
4. `Approval.device_consent` is read as `{waiting_for:'device', target_device_name?}` (contract `device-access.schema.json`).
